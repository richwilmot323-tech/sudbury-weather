How the blend works

Temperature: each source's hourly temperatures are turned into a high and low for each calendar day. The blend is the average across sources. The range shows the coolest and warmest source.

Precipitation chance: the average of the NWS probability of precipitation, the GFS and ECMWF ensembles (the share of members that give at least 0.01″ that day), and the model probabilities where a model publishes one.

Amounts: the median across sources, so one wet or dry outlier can't swing it. The range shows the spread.

Type & timing: each hour, the app counts how many sources show measurable precipitation, and of what kind. A precipitation window is any stretch where at least 40% of sources agree. Its type is the most common form during the window.

Confidence: High when sources fall within about 4° on the high and agree on whether it will precipitate. Low when they're more than 9° apart or split on precipitation.

Data: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) · Models via Open-Meteo · Radar: Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Blended forecast, not an official NWS product.
